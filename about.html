About Me

Built on strong imagery, clean execution, and honest tattooing.

A New York native and versatile shop veteran since 2010, I specialize in bold, high-movement imagery rooted in Western and Japanese traditional styles.

My work is influenced by the raw illustrative power of Frank Frazetta, alongside the technical foundations of tattooers like Ed Hardy, Brian Bruno, Danielle Hoang, and Tim Lehi.

From custom lettering and fine-tuned script to large-scale compositions in color or black and gray, I aim to translate every idea into a tattoo that hits hard and stands the test of time.

The Experience
