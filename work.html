Tattooer

Eric Saner

Custom Tattoos · Omaha, Nebraska

A New York native and versatile shop veteran since 2010, Eric specializes in bold, high-movement imagery rooted in Western and Japanese traditional styles. Having worked in tattoo shops across New York, Oakland, Seattle, and Florida, he brings a wide range of experience and influences to his work.

For Eric, tattooing is about more than the finished piece. He believes the experience matters just as much, creating a comfortable, welcoming environment where clients feel heard and involved in the creative process. Through open communication and collaboration, he works to build trust and make each tattoo a positive, personal experience.
